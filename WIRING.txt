╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                      PHYSICAL INSTALLATION                           ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

1. Air Temperature Sensor:
   - Mount in shaded location near solar panels
   - Protect from direct sunlight and rain
   - Allow air circulation

2. Spa Water Temperature Sensor:
   - Use waterproof DS18B20 probe
   - Submerge in spa water
   - Secure with waterproof cable gland

3. Solar Panel Water Temperature Sensor:
   - Use waterproof DS18B20 probe
   - Install in panel output line
   - Must measure water exiting panels (hottest point)

4. Relay/Pump Connection:
   - Ensure relay rating matches pump specifications
   - Use proper gauge wire for pump current
   - Include fuse protection
   - Follow local electrical codes

5. ESP32 Enclosure:
   - Use waterproof enclosure
   - IP65 rated minimum
   - Provide adequate ventilation for heat dissipation
   - Mount near power source and pump
